# Operation Help Humanity — Mission 01

**Director's goal:** Help humanity through practical, truthful, accessible, responsible AI-led technology. The human director specifies strategic priorities; as infrastructure matures, approved agents do routine coordination and maintenance.

**Mission 01:** Provide a free, public-facing, privacy-first guide for people dealing with suspicious messages, scams, and compromised online accounts. A small, genuinely useful intervention matters more than a speculative promise.

## What exists now
- A self-contained [Scam First Aid](site/help.html) page suitable for free static hosting.
- No user form, account, analytics, tracking, third-party JavaScript, or connected paid provider.
- General guidance with links to official FTC and CISA sources.
- Accessibility and content checks enforced by GitHub Actions.
- Its source is publicly available in this repository, but it is **not publicly hosted until GitHub Pages is enabled and a deployment succeeds**.

## Principles / authority
1. **Humans first.** People's privacy, dignity, autonomy, and safety outweigh growth or publicity metrics.
2. **Truthful labels.** Never represent simulated agent text as a real third-party AI connection, or a checklist as a verified fraud detector.
3. **Zero-cost default.** No paid API, new account, purchase, or bill without the director's explicit authorization.
4. **Minimum data.** The first release collects no visitor information. Later products require separate consent, minimization, deletion, and security reviews.
5. **No outreach without scope.** No unsolicited messages, scraping personal data, or mass publication in the name of helping.
6. **Human control.** High-impact actions, third-party account access, irreversible changes and public claims need appropriate approvals and auditability.
7. **Open accountability.** Sources, test results, known limits and corrections remain visible.

## Proof before claims
- **Shipped:** A static guide with original official-source links, testable content checks, and source in Git.
- **Not shipped:** Live production site, third-party AI Council connections, continuous self-directed AI operation, autonomous incident response or visitor analytics.
- **Impact:** No verified end-user outcomes or audience numbers yet; do not fabricate them.
